Drag platforms from your bank onto the game board. Position them strategically to create the optimal for the ball. Less platforms used rewards more stars.

Rotate platforms to change the ball's trajectory. Use the ghost trail from previous attempts to perfect your path.

Hit the drop button and watch the physics unfold. The ball will bounce according to real physics simulation.
